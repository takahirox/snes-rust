Arrows: move · Z: B · X: A · A: Y · S: X · Q/W: L/R · Enter: Start · Shift: Select

Standard gamepads are supported. Click Play to activate audio. Our sole bundled demo is the original Japanese adventure 潮鳴りの密室 / Shionari no Misshitsu. You can also open a lawfully obtained local ROM. Personal ROMs and SRAM saves stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

Battery saves persist in this browser’s local storage. Reset preserves SRAM and clears volatile memory. Loading or reloading either the demo or a local ROM starts from reset and restores its own saved SRAM.
